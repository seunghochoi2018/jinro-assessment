Terms and Disclaimer

Career Assessment is an educational exploration tool. It is not professional counseling, hiring advice, medical advice, or a guarantee of outcomes.

Use of results

Your result should be used as a starting point for reflection and research. A high match score means your current answers resemble a career profile in this system; it does not guarantee admission, employment, income, performance, or satisfaction.

Minors

Students and minors should review career decisions with a parent, teacher, counselor, or other trusted adult. The service is designed to support conversations, not replace them.

Advertising

The site may show advertising. Do not click ads to support the site. Ads should be clicked only when you have genuine interest in the advertised offer.

Accuracy

Career data, descriptions, salaries, and growth signals may change over time and may vary by country, region, school, employer, and individual background. Verify important decisions with current local sources.
