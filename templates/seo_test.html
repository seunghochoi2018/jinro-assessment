How to read the result

  1. Start with the top matched careers, but read the reasons behind the score.
  2. Compare at least three related careers so you can separate field interest from job-title familiarity.
  3. Check education, licensing, salary range, growth, and local hiring before making a major decision.
  4. Choose one small experiment, such as a course, project, interview, volunteer role, or portfolio piece.
