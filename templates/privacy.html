Privacy Policy

This page explains how Career Assessment handles basic assessment data, analytics, cookies, and advertising.

Assessment data

The assessment uses your name or nickname, age, career situation, and answers to generate career exploration results. Results are stored in the browser session while you use the site. Shared result links may include a shortened summary such as name, interest code, and top careers.

Analytics and advertising

This site may use first-party anonymous analytics, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and Google AdSense. These services may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to measure traffic, prevent fraud, and show relevant ads.

First-party analytics records events such as page views, scroll depth, time-on-page milestones, assessment section progress, and button clicks. It does not store your assessment answers, name, or raw IP address.

Cookies

Cookies and local storage may be used for language selection, session continuity, analytics, advertising, and security. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Browser games may use local storage to keep game progress such as level, gold, upgrades, stage progress, login rewards, and offline reward timing on your device.

Third parties

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons as a result of ad serving. For users in regions that require consent, a consent management solution should be enabled before personalized ads are served.
